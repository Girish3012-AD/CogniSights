**SIH26131 – Early Detection and Management of Crop Diseases and Pest Infestations

** Problem Statement
Farmers often experience significant crop losses because crop diseases and pest infestations are detected too late. Currently, many farmers depend on manual observation and local experts for disease identification, which can be time-consuming and sometimes inaccurate.
Due to delayed detection, farmers face problems such as reduced crop production, financial losses, poor crop quality, and excessive use of pesticides.
The aim of this project is to develop an **AI-powered crop disease and pest detection system** that can identify problems at an early stage and provide farmers with accurate recommendations for better crop management.

**How the Project Will Work
The proposed system, **SmartCrop AI**, will use Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to analyze crop images.

** Working Flow:
1. Image Collection
   - Farmers will upload images of affected crops or leaves through a mobile/web application.
   - The system will collect crop images along with basic information such as crop type, location, and symptoms.

2. Image Processing
   - The uploaded image will be processed using Computer Vision techniques.
   - The system will remove noise, enhance image quality, and prepare the image for analysis.

3. AI-Based Disease and Pest Detection
   - A trained Deep Learning model (such as CNN - Convolutional Neural Network) will analyze the crop image.
   - The model will identify whether the crop is healthy or affected by a specific disease or pest.
   - It will classify the type of disease/pest with accuracy.

4. Disease Prediction and Analysis
   - The system will provide details about:
     - Detected disease or pest name
     - Severity level of infection
     - Possible causes
     - Risk level for crop damage

5. Smart Recommendations
   - Based on the detection result, the system will provide farmers with suitable recommendations:
     - Prevention methods
     - Organic and chemical treatment suggestions
     - Proper pesticide usage guidance
     - Crop management tips

6. Early Warning System
   - The system can analyze environmental factors such as weather conditions and previous disease patterns.
   - It will provide early alerts to farmers about possible disease outbreaks.

7. Farmer Dashboard
   - Farmers can view:
     - Previous disease detection history
     - Crop health reports
     - Treatment recommendations
     - Future prevention suggestions

***Technologies Used:
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Machine Learning / Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)
- Image Processing
- Mobile/Web Application

***Expected Outcome:
SmartCrop AI will help farmers detect crop diseases and pest attacks at an early stage, reduce crop losses, minimize unnecessary pesticide usage, improve productivity, and support better decision-making in agriculture.
