** SIH26094 - AI Powered Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring and Distress Prediction System

** Problem Overview
Mental health problems such as stress, anxiety, and depression are increasing among students, working professionals, and society.
Many people fail to identify their mental health problems at an early stage due to hesitation, lack of awareness, and absence of continuous monitoring.
This project aims to develop an AI-powered system that monitors emotional patterns and predicts mental distress levels using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

**Objective
The main objectives of this project are:
- Monitor user's emotional patterns
- Detect stress and distress levels at an early stage
- Analyze user feelings using AI and NLP
- Provide personalized mental wellness suggestions
