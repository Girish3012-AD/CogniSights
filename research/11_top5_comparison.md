This matrix provides a deep comparison of the top 5 finalists to highlight the trade-offs between Impact, Feasibility, and SIH Competitiveness.

| Dimension | SIH26001 (Landslides) | SIH26011 (3D ULPIN) | SIH26003 (Dementia App) | SIH26002 (NER Logistics) | SIH26009 (Manganese) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

### Trade-off Analysis
- **SIH26001 (Landslides)** offers the best human-impact story, but it is risky because the software cannot function without a physical sensor network, which a software hackathon team cannot build.
- **SIH26011 (3D ULPIN)** offers the best systemic impact as it lays the foundation for future digital public infrastructure (DPI), similar to UPI or Aadhaar. However, processing 3D spatial data requires specialized skills.
- **SIH26003 (Dementia)** is the safest bet for execution. A skilled app development team can easily build a beautiful, functional prototype. However, it lacks "deep tech" wow-factor.
- **SIH26009 (Manganese)** is the ultimate "moonshot". If successful, it is a guaranteed winner due to its sheer technical difficulty, but the risk of total failure during development is extremely high.
