## SIH26001: AI-Based early warning and landslide Risk Monitoring System in NER
- **Organization**: Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region
- **Theme**: Disaster Management
- **Real-world impact**: 95/100
- **Solution opportunity**: 90/100
- **Systemic leverage**: 85/100
- **Future potential**: 90/100
- **Technical feasibility**: 75/100
- **Scalability**: 80/100
- **SIH competitiveness**: 95/100
- **Evidence confidence**: HIGH
- **Why it is Top**: Directly impacts human lives by preventing landslide fatalities.
- **Why it beats the alternatives**: Addresses a life-or-death scenario with clear metrics for success compared to administrative optimizations.
- **Strongest evidence**: The NER heavily suffers from landslides annually.
- **Weakest assumption**: Assumes remote sensor and IoT data will be reliably available in low-connectivity areas.
- **Existing solutions**: Reactive manual reporting.
- **Unsolved gap**: Proactive, predictive analysis integrating GIS and real-time alerts.
- **Potential solution direction**: GIS-integrated predictive analytics engine using satellite and weather API data.
- **Potential second-order effects**: Reduces economic loss from road blockages and supply chain disruption.
- **Biggest risk**: Lack of reliable input data (soil moisture sensors).
- **Why we might be wrong**: The hardware deployment (sensors) may be the bottleneck, making the software useless.
