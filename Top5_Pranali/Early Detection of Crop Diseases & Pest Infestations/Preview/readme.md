Selected SIH 2026 Problem Statements

🥇 1st Choice — 26131

Crop Disease & Pest Early Detection
This is our first preference because it has strong AI/ML scope, high agricultural impact, good scalability, and can be developed as a strong software-based solution using Computer Vision, ML, weather data and risk prediction.

🥈 2nd Choice — 26033

Agricultural Supply Chain & Direct Marketplace
This is our second preference because it is highly relevant to farmers and consumers and provides strong opportunities for AI demand forecasting, supply-demand matching and route optimization. It is also more feasible for a CSE/Data Science team than a hardware-heavy solution.

🥉 3rd Choice — 26134
Industry Skill Development & Job-Market Alignment
A good AI/software problem involving skill-gap analysis, industry-demand prediction and recommendation, but it has comparatively less distinctive impact than our top three.

4th Choice — 26154
Gen AI Content Transformation
Interesting and easy to demonstrate, but GenAI content-generation platforms are already common, so achieving strong differentiation would be difficult.

5th Choice — 26032
Farmer Procurement Management
Useful for reducing waiting time and improving procurement-centre management, but the solution is comparatively simpler and has less AI/innovation scope.
