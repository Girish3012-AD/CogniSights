1. Problem Statement Details
   
Problem Statement ID: 26032

Organization: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

Problem Statement Title: Farmers often face long waiting times, lack of information regarding procurement schedules, and uncertainty about procurement status.

Department: Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA)

Category: Software

Theme: Heritage & Culture

2. Problem Statement
Farmers visiting procurement centres may face long waiting times, overcrowding and lack of information about their procurement schedule, queue position, procurement status and payment status.
The problem is to provide farmers with a digital system for efficient procurement-centre management.

3. Proposed Solution
Develop a Digital Procurement Slot & Queue Management System that allows farmers to:
Register digitally
Book procurement slots
Track their queue position
Receive SMS/app notifications
Track procurement status
Track payment status

4. System Workflow

Farmer Registration → Slot Booking → Queue Tracking → Procurement → Payment Tracking

5. Main Features
Farmer Registration
Slot Booking
Real-Time Queue Management
SMS/App Notifications
Procurement Status Tracking
Payment Status Tracking
Admin Dashboard

6. Target Users
Farmers
Procurement-centre staff
Government authorities

7. Expected Benefits
Reduces farmer waiting time
Reduces congestion at procurement centres
Provides transparency
Improves procurement-centre management
Makes payment tracking easier
Provides farmers with timely updates

8. Suggested Technology
Frontend: React.js
Backend: Python + FastAPI
Database: MySQL/PostgreSQL
Notifications: SMS/Email/Push Notifications
Authentication: JWT
Dashboard: React + Chart.js/Recharts

9. Core Idea

A digital platform that allows farmers to book procurement slots, track their queue and procurement process, receive notifications, and monitor their payment status without unnecessary waiting at procurement centres.

10. One-Line SIH Pitch

“Our solution digitizes the farmer procurement process through smart slot booking, real-time queue tracking, notifications and payment tracking, reducing waiting time and improving transparency at procurement centres.”
