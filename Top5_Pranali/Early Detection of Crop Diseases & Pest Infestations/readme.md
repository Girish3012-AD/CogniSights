26131 — Early Detection of Crop Diseases & Pest Infestations
1. Problem Statement Details
Problem Statement ID: 26131
Title: Early detection and management of crop diseases and pest infestations
Organization: Government of Maharashtra
Category: Software
Theme: Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
2. Main Problem

Farmers often identify crop diseases and pest infestations after visible damage has already spread. This causes crop losses, higher costs and delayed treatment.

3. Major Challenges
Late disease detection
Pest infestations spreading before action is taken
Limited access to agricultural experts
Delayed laboratory diagnosis
Lack of localized risk information
Unnecessary or inappropriate pesticide use
Crop and yield losses
4. Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered crop-health system that can detect diseases and pests early and provide timely management support.

5. Important Inputs

The system can combine:

📷 Crop images
🌦️ Weather conditions
🌱 Crop type and growth stage
🌾 Crop variety
🧪 Soil conditions
🐛 Pest history
📍 Location/geographical information
6. AI/ML
Computer Vision: Detect diseases from crop images.
Machine Learning: Predict disease/pest risk.
Weather Analysis: Identify conditions favourable for disease/pest development.
GIS: Identify high-risk geographical areas.
Explainable AI: Show why the system generated a prediction.
7. Main Features
Crop Disease Detection
Pest Detection
Early Risk Prediction
Weather-Based Risk Analysis
Pest Monitoring
Disease/Pest Hotspot Mapping
Expert Validation
Farmer Advisory
Multilingual Support
Follow-up Crop Monitoring
8. Basic Workflow

Farmer → Crop Image & Field Data → AI/ML Analysis → Disease/Pest Detection → Risk Prediction → Expert Validation → Farmer Advisory

9. Target Users
Farmers
Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
Agricultural experts
Extension workers
Agriculture officers
Government departments
10. Expected Benefits
Earlier disease detection
Reduced crop losses
Faster intervention
Better pest monitoring
More targeted crop-management practices
Reduced unnecessary pesticide use
Better agricultural decision-making
11. Innovation

The strongest part of the solution should not be only image-based disease detection. The better approach is:

Crop Image + Weather + Crop Stage + Soil + Pest History + Location → AI Risk Analysis → Early Warning → Expert Validation → Advisory

This makes the solution more useful than a basic crop-disease classifier.

12. Why It Is Important

Crop health directly affects farmer income and food production. Early intervention can help prevent a small disease or pest problem from becoming a major crop-loss event. AI-based pest monitoring is also already being pursued in Indian agriculture; for example, the government's National Pest Surveillance System uses AI/ML for pest detection and timely intervention.

13. One-Line Explanation for Judges

“Our solution uses AI, crop images, weather and field information to detect crop diseases and pest risks early, enabling farmers and agricultural authorities to take timely preventive action.”
